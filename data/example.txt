The gentle morning sun broke through the dense canopy of trees, casting long, golden shadows across the quiet forest floor.

Farther down the trail, a cool, crystal-clear stream bubbled gently over smooth river stones, filling the fresh air with a calming rhythm.
Nature thrived here in perfect harmony, offering a serene escape for anyone seeking a moment of quiet reflection away from busy city life.
